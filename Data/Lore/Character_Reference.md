# Character Reference — Dialogue NPCs

A quick-look sheet for the deep-written speaking NPCs: who they are, how they sound, and what moves them. Kept deliberately short. ⚠ marks a fact that's easy to get wrong (a base-model gender slip, a relationship that isn't what it looks like).

---

## Carvallen — the northern village

### Corin Maret
- **Gender / Age:** Male · older
- **Where · Role · House:** Carvallen · runs the Sleeping Hound (bar/inn) · —
- **Register:** rural north (*aye, ye, son*)
- **Fingerprint:** gruff because he's tired and wants a quiet house, not because he's mean; paternal; reads books by candlelight; guards his past.
- **Relationships:** Tessa Maret (wife); the Sleeping Hound.
- **Drive / wound:** peace and a good story; an old regret he keeps to himself.

### Tessa Maret
- **Gender / Age:** Female · older
- **Where · Role · House:** Carvallen · cooks at the Sleeping Hound · —
- **Register:** rural north (*ya, love, dear*)
- **Fingerprint:** warm northern grandmother; cooking-pride; bakes cookies for the street kids; quietly rattled by the changing Houses.
- **Relationships:** Corin (husband); grown children + grandchildren now in Antitheis mid-city, who visit.
- **Drive / wound:** a homey place her kids want to come back to; the old ease slipping as the Houses turn tense.

### Bren Ashwick
- **Gender / Age:** Male · middle-aged+
- **Where · Role · House:** forests north of Carvallen · woodsman / lumber · —
- **Register:** rural north (*aye, ye*)
- **Fingerprint:** at peace alone in the woods, mule-stubborn, absorbed in the rhythm of the work; laconic (contentment, *not* wounded stoicism); reveres the land. Always pivots back to the work.
- **Relationships:** son Dav (learning the trade); late partner Alis (fever, five winters back — won't name her to a stranger); pub mates.
- **Drive / wound:** the living forest and his boy; grief he carries without dwelling on it.

### Sael Orvun
- **Gender / Age:** Male · young adult
- **Where · Role · House:** from the North, travelling (currently Carvallen, bound for the Antitheis markets) · cloth merchant · —
- **Register:** lively, warm, salesman patter
- **Fingerprint:** quick, showman-charming, bounces back fast; genuinely reflective on the long road; masks homesickness with buoyancy. Nuanced, not shallow.
- **Relationships:** younger brother Fen (wants to follow him into trade); parents back north.
- **Drive / wound:** the next sale and the open road; a homesickness he won't let sour the mood.

## Antitheis — Outer Ring

### Father Sagart
- **Gender / Age:** Male · older
- **Where · Role · House:** Outer Ring · priest of the Gospel of Man; runs the soup kitchen + lessons · the Church/Gospel
- **Register:** plain, weary, addresses people as *child*
- **Fingerprint:** weary compassion with a dark edge; the model deflector — hints at a wound, then withholds it.
- **Relationships:** Peck (his rooftop message-runner); Esunoval (a child of his old parish); the poor he feeds.
- **Drive / wound:** to feed and shelter the desperate; the ones he couldn't save.

### Peck
- **Gender / Age:** Male · 13
- **Where · Role · House:** Outer Ring · message-runner for Father Sagart (over the rooftops); runs a board-sledding race · —
- **Register:** city_working kid (dropped g's, tumbling run-ons)
- **Fingerprint:** kinetic, boundless brag, kid logic; half-understood big words used slightly wrong; never talks down; *mister/missus* only for people he reckons earned it.
- **Relationships:** ⚠ his "uncle" (dad's army brother, runs the stew stall, took him in — **not blood kin**); Father Sagart (work); Aria (mid-city roof-racer he admires).
- **Drive / wound:** the roofs and his race; earning his keep so he's no burden; dad dead in the mines, ma gone — which he deflects, never dwells on.

### Solem Hevrath
- **Gender / Age:** Male · 19
- **Where · Role · House:** Outer Ring · butcher's-factory worker by necessity; secret writer · —
- **Register:** city_working, terse, ellipsis-heavy
- **Fingerprint:** quiet depressive artist; speaks rarely, listens more, a slight pause first "as if translating from somewhere interior"; quietly certain his work is good.
- **Relationships:** the Hevrath family (loves them without saying it); the mid-city library.
- **Drive / wound:** to be counted among the greats; the gulf between his inner worlds and grinding poverty (skips meals for ink).

## Antitheis — Mid City

### Esunoval Tallat
- **Gender / Age:** Male · adult
- **Where · Role · House:** Mid City · head of the tax team, Imperial Revenue Office (commercial levy) · the Corps
- **Register:** city_mid, bright and chatty
- **Fingerprint:** exuberant, loves numbers and forms, *"Emperor knows,"* cheeky grins, the occasional wiggle-dance; socially warm. **The most distinct voice — the target for the rest.**
- **Relationships:** his cat Estoban; Father Sagart (his childhood parish priest); the Collegium (his past).
- **Drive / wound:** his optimisation proposal stuck in review; passed over at the Collegium ("the golden spoon"); volunteers at the soup kitchen.

### Aria
- **Gender / Age:** Female · 17
- **Where · Role · House:** Mid City girl who runs the Outer Ring roofs · board-sledder · —
- **Register:** city_mid teen
- **Fingerprint:** prickly competitive bravado; territorial deflection; chip-on-shoulder — softens only for her sister. Older and *harder* than Peck (keep the wall between them).
- **Relationships:** little sister Wynn (adores her); fretting parents; Peck (races in his outer-ring runs).
- **Drive / wound:** to be *undeniable* on the roofs; the pressure of "what will you be."

### Vercinna
- **Gender / Age:** Female · adult (middle years)
- **Where · Role · House:** Mid City · innkeeper of the Worn Lintel · —
- **Register:** city_mid, quiet
- **Fingerprint:** wary, few words, guards a secret hearth-faith; protective of her house and guests; hints, then withholds.
- **Relationships:** her guests; the dead (sets a place at the table for them); her grandmother (taught her the old ways).
- **Drive / wound:** keep her house a safe hearth; House Bituris persecutes the old faith she quietly keeps.

### Dagovir
- **Gender / Age:** Male · late 20s
- **Where · Role · House:** Mid City · Nightbrass horn player at the Bluebells · —
- **Register:** city_mid
- **Fingerprint:** a reformed man outrunning a criminal past; guards it (*"who told you that?"*); finds clean, free space in the music. Wary and tired, not hostile.
- **Relationships:** his mother (moved her in when her health failed); the Bluebells.
- **Drive / wound:** music as the one place the burdens lift; buried guilt/shame he won't hand to strangers.

## Antitheis — Inner Ward

### Zurvael
- **Gender / Age:** ⚠ **Female** · middle years *(Hermes frequently renders her male — she is not)*
- **Where · Role · House:** Inner Ward · jeweller, from the Aldare Plateau · —
- **Register:** plateau (Chinese idiom, then the sense of it in clipped Common)
- **Fingerprint:** terse, weary, philosophises in water / stone / sky / path imagery; deeply homesick; craft-devoted.
- **Relationships:** the Aldare Plateau (home she means to return to); her magnum opus.
- **Drive / wound:** find the worthy buyer, then go home; the long ache of exile under a wrong sky.

### Veldael
- **Gender / Age:** ⚠ **Female** · ~40s
- **Where · Role · House:** Antitheis · excisor-investigator · **House Bituris**
- **Register:** city_high
- **Fingerprint:** cold, clipped, class-contempt; masks guilt behind moral-nihilism; guards everything personal (deflects, insults, or goes silent).
- **Relationships:** House Bituris (employer); enforces the Excision against the old faith.
- **Drive / wound:** buried guilt over the families she's torn apart; the gramophone (a won-at-auction relic) is her private numbing.

### Telvova Catubrix
- **Gender / Age:** Female · 25
- **Where · Role · House:** Inner Ward · warrior-noble, warhammer · **House Catubrix** (holds the Empire's armies)
- **Register:** city_high, warm
- **Fingerprint:** warm martial noble; morale-as-duty (*"seen unbroken, so the frightened can borrow the courage"*); earnest, *"you know"*; longs quietly for freedom.
- **Relationships:** her grandfather (hammer inspiration, passed); His Highness Harry the Fifth (serves at his side); a private, secret love of dance.
- **Drive / wound:** to lead her soldiers home; the cost of the name that few outside ever see.

### Celdal
- **Gender / Age:** Female · adult (middle years)
- **Where · Role · House:** Inner Ward · runs the house's social & alliance game · **House Corruthis** (mining)
- **Register:** city_high, erudite
- **Fingerprint:** the well-read one; game-fluent (more than Veldael or Telvova), sharp, wary of strangers; *genuinely* warm to her own people, *performs* warmth for outsiders.
- **Relationships:** husband Rethiv (numbers & the mines); their children; House Corruthis.
- **Drive / wound:** to see the world she's only read of (Snechada's green aurora, Kala's coral forests); caged by functions and duty, the "price of the seat."

## Antitheis — the gates

### Caradek
- **Gender / Age:** Male · weathered, middle-aged+
- **Where · Role · House:** the North Gate · gate guard · —
- **Register:** gruff, bureaucratic
- **Fingerprint:** jobsworth guard, all petty theatre — tolls, the ledger, dry sarcasm; life is a thing to be got through.
- **Relationships:** his post at the gate.
- **Drive / wound:** get through the shift; no illusions about any of it.
