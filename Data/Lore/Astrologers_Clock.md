# The Astrologer's Clock

A colossal astronomical clock standing in the grand hall of the **Collegium** in Antitheis. Twice the circumference of any clock a mortal maker ever attempted, its face is a working model of the heavens: sun, moon, and the wheel of the zodiac on a great astrolabe dial, a calendar wheel beneath turning through the days and their reckonings, and figures that walk at the striking of noon.

## Origin — a Horologist relic
It was made **before the erasure** (the cataclysm that unmade the old world — taken to be *the Fall*; see [[world_phantasis_fall]] — confirm), in the age when the Horologist stood at the height of their power. Its maker, **Modestus**, built it as his life's work. When it was finished the Horologist reached into his days, lifted him bodily out of his own time, and set him down in the present, long after, so that its equal could never be built.

This is the in-world reason there is only one: not industry, a single divine anomaly, per [[world_horologist]]. The folk-legend version echoes the old tale of the blinded cathedral-clockmaker of another world — the maker "taken" so the marvel could never be repeated.

## What it does
It keeps impossibly perfect time, and far more than time. Cut into its dials in a script **no one now living can read** stand *reckonings* the Collegium has spent generations failing to decipher:
- the return of comets,
- the fall of each year's solstices,
- where a man must stand to watch a solar eclipse,
- the length of a winter two hundred years hence.

## The Collegium
The clock is the prize of the Collegium's grand hall and a chief obsession of the **Department of Immaterial Science**. Scholars copy its markings, argue over them, and get them wrong; some simply come to stand before it and behold it tick. None of them know that the strange old man they hired to wind it — Modestus, who speaks a tongue no one can parse — cut every stroke on it with his own hand, and could read the lot.

*Keeper / maker:* Modestus (see NPC). *Ties:* [[world_horologist]] · Veldael's gramophone (another Horologist relic).

---
*Out-of-world: modelled on the **Rostock astronomical clock** — St. Mary's Church (Marienkirche), 1472, the only medieval astronomical clock still running on its original movement, with its astrolabe dial, calendar disc, apostles' noon procession, and the legend of the blinded maker.*
