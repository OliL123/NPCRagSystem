# Name Pool — draw from these

Unassigned names to hand out to new NPCs. Drawn from Gaulish, Welsh/Breton/Cornish, Gaelic, and southern (Latin/Occitan/Iberian) records, deliberately varied in prefix, ending, and length. Endings `-doc / -rec / -ran / -in` were avoided on purpose. **When you use one, move it from here into [Name_Bank.md](Name_Bank.md).**

---

## First names (≈200)

> **Two axes when picking a name:** (1) *gender read* — the buckets below are how a name lands on most modern ears, and a few are treacherous (see ⚠); (2) *generation feel* — match an elder to a weighty old name, a youth to a light one.
>
> **⚠ Reads ambiguous / not what its bucket says — verify before assigning:** **Emlyn** (reads *female* to many; the one that burned us — freed but handle with care), Delwyn, Osian, Meredydd, Loeiza. Treat the whole **Either** list as needs-a-second-look too.
>
> **Reads elder / weighty** (older NPCs) — *m:* Aurelian · Berengar · Baldric · Amalric · Alaric · Savaric · Herve · Crispus · Ysgawn · *f:* Hodierna · Ermessende · Comitissa · Placida · Verecunda · Ermeline.
> **Reads young / light** (youths) — *m:* Cai · Deri · Alen · Iestyn · Emrys · Aeron · Teilo · *f:* Ffion · Gwenno · Nesta · Lowri · Eirlys · Seren.
